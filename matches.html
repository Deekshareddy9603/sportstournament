Tournament Roadmap

Follow the official seasonal schedule from the initial open registration phases all the way to the Grand Finals arena.

Phase 1: Oct 1 - Oct 15

Open Registration & Team Formation

Captains assemble their rosters, link their gaming IDs, and submit their official squad names for bracket seeding.

Current Phase: Oct 20 - Nov 10

Group Stages & Point Accumulation

Round-robin format matches begin. Teams battle in intense weekly fixtures to secure maximum points on the live leaderboard.

Playoff Elimination Bracket
